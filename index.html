Chemistry & medicine

Neurons talk to each other with chemicals called neurotransmitters. Many brain medicines work by raising, lowering, copying or blocking one of them.

How a synapse works

  1. An action potential arrives at the axon terminal.
  2. Calcium (Ca²⁺) flows in and triggers vesicles to release neurotransmitter.
  3. The neurotransmitter crosses the synapse and binds receptors on the next cell.
  4. The signal ends when the neurotransmitter is broken down by enzymes or taken back up (reuptake).

Medicine names are examples for study. Never take or change a medicine without a doctor.
